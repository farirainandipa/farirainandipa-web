1. About AfSAE and AAS7
The African Society of Association Executives (AfSAE) is Africa’s leading membership body
for association management professionals. Through knowledge sharing, professional
development, and continental advocacy, AfSAE strengthens the capacity of associations to
serve their members and communities across Africa.
The Africa Associations Summit (AAS) is AfSAE’s flagship annual event—Africa’s premier
gathering for association management professionals. Now in its seventh edition, AAS7 will
bring together 150–200 delegates from across the continent and beyond for two days of
insight, networking, and collaboration.
AfSAE invites proposals from cities across Africa to serve as the host of AAS7. The selected
city will partner with AfSAE to deliver a world-class summit that showcases African
hospitality, infrastructure, and cultural richness.

3. Scope of Work
The selected host city is expected to provide or facilitate the following:
3.1 Conference Venue
• A single main plenary hall accommodating 150-200 attendees with comfortable
theatre or classroom-style seating.
• Exhibition/networking space for sponsors, partners, and exhibitors.
• State-of-the-art audiovisual equipment, including projection, microphones, and stage
lighting.
• High-speed, reliable Wi-Fi accessible throughout the venue.
• Dedicated registration/welcome area at the entrance.
• On-site catering facilities or approved catering services for meals and refreshments.
3.2 Accommodation
• Preferred hotel partners offering a range of 3– to 5-star accommodation options.
• Negotiated room block rates for AAS7 delegates.
• Proximity to the conference venue (within a reasonable distance or complimentary
shuttle provided).
3.3 Transportation & Accessibility
• Accessibility via major international airports with direct or connecting flights from key
African hubs.
• Arrangements for airport transfers (shuttle services or coordinated transportation).
• Local transportation options for delegates, including shuttles between the venue,
hotels, and any off-site events.
3.4 City Support & Hospitality
• Active promotion of AAS7 locally and internationally, including through the city’s
tourism and convention bureau.
• Logistical support, including assistance with permits, security arrangements, and
government liaison where required.
• Organisation of cultural programming or site visits that reflect the city’s heritage and
character.
• A welcome reception or social event for delegates, ideally sponsored or co-
sponsored by the host city.
3.5 Financial Considerations
• A comprehensive cost breakdown covering venue rental, accommodation, catering,
AV/technical equipment, and support services.
• Details of any subvention, grant, or in-kind contribution available from the city,
convention bureau, or government.
• A commitment to actively facilitate local sponsorships to offset event costs, including
venue, AV, catering, and the welcome reception.

4. Proposal Requirements
Proposals must address each of the following sections. Clear, concise responses supported
by evidence, images, or data are strongly encouraged.
4.1 City Overview
• A brief description of the city’s appeal, accessibility, and relevance to AAS7.
• A summary of comparable international or regional events previously hosted
(including scale and year).
4.2 Venue Details
• Name and description of the proposed venue, including capacity and layout options.
• Images, floor plans, or virtual tour links.
• Written confirmation of availability in October/November 2026.
4.3 Accommodation Options
• A list of recommended hotels with star ratings, distance from the venue, and
estimated room rates.
• Confirmation of ability to offer a room block arrangement for AAS7 delegates.
4.4 Transportation Plan
• Details of international airport connectivity, including key African routes served.
• Proposed local transportation plan for delegates during the event.
4.5 Financial Proposal & Support
• An itemised budget estimate covering all costs under the host city’s scope.
• Subvention amount or in-kind support offered (if any).
• Strategy for securing local sponsorships to reduce costs for AfSAE.
4.6 Sustainability Commitment
• A description of the city’s or venue’s approach to sustainable event practices.
• Any relevant certifications, green initiatives, or environmental commitments.
4.7 Proposed Preparation Timeline
• A high-level timeline from contract signing through to post-event wrap-up.
